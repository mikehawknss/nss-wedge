How the number is built

Drift (squares) = 0.5 × dots × (side wind in mph ÷ 10), with exact mph (m/s × 2.237). The side part is wind × sin(angle off the aim line) and the head/tail part is wind × cos(angle). Each power dot carries about 12 squares (12 to 13 in the game). Power between the dots is assumed to scale linearly, and head/tail wind has no effect until you enter a rate. The grid is one square per cell (16 wide, 60 tall), with a darker line every 5. The wind disc is redrawn from in-game captures.
